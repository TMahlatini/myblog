Essentially, this site is a digital notebook. I use this space to explore anything I find interesting or worth reflecting on; as such, some essays will be more polished than others. I am always open to a good conversation, so please feel free to say hello at: hello@terencemahlatini.com.  

**Quick facts about me**

I was born and raised in Gweru, Zimbabwe. Last May, I graduated from Whitman College with a B.A. in Computer Science. Currently, I am based in Illinois, USA, working at MulticoreWare.

**Why I am writing here?** 

Writing helps organize my thoughts, preserve memories, and generate new ideas. Most importantly, it gives my future self a window into the past, a way to trace the origins of whatever chaos might be manifesting then.

I have chosen to host these thoughts on the open web partly as a rebellion against the social media format, and it feels good having some real estate on the internet.
