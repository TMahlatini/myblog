It demands attention, discipline and organization. 

When I built this website my goal was to increase my writing output but I have struggled with consistency. My initial target was publishing ***long*** essays weekly but it seems that might have been a little ambitious. 

The biggest hurdle here has been "*long*". Instead of aiming for something lengthy and polished, I am going to prioritize consistency first. That means short and rugged.
