### Thinking, Fast and Slow Chpt 22: 

This chapter tries to answer the question: When can you trust a self-confident professional who claims to have an intuition? 

The answer to that is another question: Is the professional in an environment that is sufficiently regular and provides validity of intuitive judgements? 

"Claims of correct intuitions in an unpredictable situation are self-delusional at best, sometimes worse. In the absence of valid cues, intuitive 'hits' are due to either luck or to lies.....intuition cannot be trusted in the absence of stable regularities in the environment." 

Essentially intuition is pattern recognition. You are better off trusting an expert chess player's intuition over any chess configuration than any political pundit forecasting the future.
