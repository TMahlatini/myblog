I have always found it hard to articulate my relationship with God up until I read the poem below. It was written by Trust Shoko, a close friend, and the first time I read it I couldn’t believe how much it resonated with me. It’s so accurate I don’t feel like I can add or subtract anything, nor can I elaborate any further, at least at the moment. This poem is part of a collection which you can find <a href="https://www.amazon.com/stores/author/B0G6RZ44ST/allbooks?ccs_id=63130ba4-3849-453b-91b4-b0efa0a2c8b4" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">here</a> along with the rest of his work and you can find his <a href="https://linktr.ee/trustmunopa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">linktree here.</a> 

```
Kátharsis

It all started with opening my eye during prayer 
  that I saw damnation flash past my sight.
  It didn't look like the devil and horns, 
  but like the comfort of opening both of them.
  So I did.
  It was like the feeling that God and I are alike, 
  and we can see eye to eye
  Literally.


It'll all end
  with me forcing my eyes shut again,
  to unsee the forbidden
  and be a creation to my Creator
  to let go, into the discomfort of the brawl
  between the flesh and the spirit 
  for God and I to see eye to eye, 
  literally.

```
